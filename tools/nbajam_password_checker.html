      How passwords work

      After each game, the stats screen shows a 14-character password with your record: wins and losses (up to 255 each), your current win streak, and which teams you've beaten. To load it, start a game, choose Yes to enter initials, type your initials, then type the password.

      A password only works with the initials it was made for, because the initials are part of its key. If it's rejected, the game says No record and starts you from scratch.
